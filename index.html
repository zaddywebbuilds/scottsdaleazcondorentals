02 — The Experience

A Day Here

A day in the Valley of the Sun

From sunrise over the McDowells to city lights across the desert floor — this is what a day looks like when Scottsdale is your address.

Morning

Coffee, cactus wrens, 70 degrees.

Step onto your balcony as the sun clears the McDowell Mountains. The community is still quiet, the pool untouched, the air dry and clean. Trailheads are ten minutes away — and you haven't even left the property.

  • Sunrise over the McDowell and Camelback ranges
  • Heated community pool before the day warms
  • Full kitchen — breakfast the way you make it at home
  • Hiking at Pinnacle Peak, Tom's Thumb and Camelback nearby

Afternoon

Golf, shopping, or nothing at all.

Tee off at TPC Scottsdale, Troon North or the Kierland course along the 9th hole. Or browse Kierland Commons and Scottsdale Quarter, then return to a lounge chair beside the water feature.

  • Championship golf minutes from every community
  • Kierland Commons & Scottsdale Quarter shopping
  • Community fitness centers and lap pools
  • Old Town galleries, spas and the Arizona Canal

Sunset

The hour the desert turns rose.

Arizona sunsets are not a cliché — they are the reason people move here. Watch the ridgelines burn pink from your patio, then slip into the spa as the temperature drops and the stars arrive.

  • Patios and balconies oriented for the evening light
  • Year-round hot tubs and heated spas
  • Barbecue and outdoor dining in every community
  • Hot-air balloon flights over the north Valley

Evening

Dinner in Old Town. Home in fifteen.

The Valley's best tables are a short drive in any direction — then back through a gated entry to a quiet, secure community and a home that is entirely yours.

  • Gated entries with privacy and security
  • Cable television, DVD and high-speed connectivity
  • Fine dining across Old Town, Kierland and Cave Creek
  • City lights from the elevated north Valley communities
